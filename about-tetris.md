Tetris was created in Soviet Moscow in 1984. 
It's one of the most successful video games in history. 

</br>
It was invented by a software engineer (Alexey Pajitnov) on an Elektronika 60 computer. 
</br> He combined geometric math with his favorite sport. (I didn't know that before)
</br>
</br> Today, the game has sold over 520 million copies across dozens of platforms.

</br> The name "Tetris" is a mashup of "tetra" (the Greek numerical prefix for four, 
</br> as all game pieces are made of four blocks) </br> and "tennis," Pajitnov's favorite sport.
